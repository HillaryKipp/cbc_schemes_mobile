# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated:** September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **CBC Schemes of Work** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). 

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a professional lesson planning and scheme generator experience for Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) educators, we may collect the following types of information:

### A. Personal Information & Authentication Data
- **Account Information:** When you sign in to the App using **Google Sign-In**, we receive your basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) provided by Google and authenticated securely via our backend service (**Supabase**).

### B. User-Generated Content & App Data
- **Schemes of Work and Lesson Plans:** The lesson plans, schemes of work, teaching notes, and educational data you create, edit, or generate within the App are stored securely. Depending on your usage, these may be saved locally on your device (via secure local storage) or synced with our secure cloud database.
- **App Usage & Preferences:** We store user preferences (such as subject choices, grade levels, and app settings) to improve your user experience.

### C. Device & Technical Information
- **Diagnostic & Log Data:** We may collect standard technical logs, crash reports, and performance data to diagnose issues and ensure app stability.
- **Internet Access:** The App requires internet access to authenticate your account, sync cloud data, and fetch educational templates.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To provide, maintain, and improve the App's lesson planning and scheme generation features.
- To authenticate your identity and secure your account using Google Sign-In and Supabase.
- To enable PDF export and document generation features.
- To respond to your support requests, feedback, or inquiries.
- To monitor and analyze usage trends to enhance app performance and reliability.
